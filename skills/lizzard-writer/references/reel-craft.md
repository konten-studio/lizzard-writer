# Reel craft

How the voice-over is actually written. Studied from Indonesian explainer reels that run this format well (consumer-rights explainers, ~80s, talking head + text). The rules below are the pattern, not a copy of anyone's words.

## The 7 beats (60-90s version)

| Beat | Time | What happens | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Scene | 0-8s | Drop the viewer **inside the bad moment**, present tense, second person | "[Person you love] lagi [situation]. Terus [authority] bilang, '[the scary line]'." |
| 2. Stakes | 8-14s | Put a price on it: money, health, time, family. One concrete number | "[Option A] berarti [pain]. [Option B] bisa [cost]. Lo bingung." |
| 3. Padahal | 14-26s | Flip the belief. Name the source out loud | "Padahal [source] udah negesin, [the truth]." |
| 4. Open loop | 26-32s | Promise **one small thing**, held to the end | "Dan ada satu [pertanyaan / kertas / angka / gerakan] yang [big effect]. Gue kasih tahu di akhir." |
| 5. Steps | 32-60s | 3 numbered, physical actions | "Pertama, ... Kedua, ... Ketiga, ..." Each with a reason in one line |
| 6. Twist | 60-72s | Close the loop. The one thing is tiny | "[Pertanyaan] yang tadi: '...'" Then one line on why it works |
| 7. Share CTA | last 5-8s | Send it to **a specific person**, then follow | "Kirim ini ke [specific person] yang [specific moment]. Dan follow, biar makin banyak yang lo tahu." |

**30-45s version:** Scene+Stakes (0-7s) → Padahal (7-12s) → Open loop (12-15s) → 2-3 levers fast → Twist → CTA. Or the list format: "N kebiasaan kecil ini..." with the last item held as the twist ("dan nomor 5 yang paling sering").

## Danger vs happy

- **Danger** is the default for this format: a scene the viewer fears being in, then relief. Every danger reel must end with what to do.
- **Happy** uses the same skeleton with a desire scene: "Lo pengen [win], tapi kirain harus [big effort]. Padahal cukup [tiny lever]." The "padahal" beat becomes "ternyata nggak perlu [big effort]".

## Sentence rules

- One idea per sentence. **2-4 seconds spoken** (≈6-12 words). Short lines are easier to subtitle and harder to scroll past.
- Present tense, second person in organic content ("lo/kamu/Bapak-Ibu" to fit the audience).
- Spoken words, not written ones: "gak", "udah", "bilang", "nanggung".
- Concrete nouns: a person ("ibu lo"), a place ("meja pengaduan"), a document ("surat pulang"), a number ("6 bulan", "165"). No "pola hidup sehat", no "berbagai faktor".
- Quote the scary line as people actually say it ("jatah BPJS-nya cuma 3 hari, Pak").
- Name the source in the voice-over when there is one (regulation, official body, guideline). Put full references in the caption.
- No intros, no "halo guys", no "di video kali ini". The first word is already the scene.

## The "one small thing"

The open loop is what holds the middle. It must be:
- **One**, singular: one question, one paper, one number, one movement.
- **Physically small**: a sentence you can say, a paper you can hold, 1 minute you can spend.
- **Delivered**, exactly as promised, near the end. Never "link in bio" for the payoff.

## On-screen text

- Hook text 0-3s: ≤7 words, the stake, big.
- Each step gets a number + 2-4 words ("1. Jangan tanda tangan").
- The twist line on screen in quotes, so people screenshot it.
- Subtitles on for everything; most people watch muted.

## Caption pattern

```
[Scene as a question]? [Don't-do-X-yet emoji ✋]

Ada 1 [thing] yang [effect]. [Saya/Gue] kasih tahu di akhir video, tonton sampai habis 👀

Follow [@handle] & share ke [specific person].

#tag #tag #tag

—
Sumber: [regulation / guideline / expert], one per line
```

## Pre-ship checklist

- [ ] First line is a scene or a stake, not a greeting
- [ ] A real number or named source appears before 20s
- [ ] Open loop promises ONE small thing and the reel delivers it
- [ ] 3 steps max, each a physical action
- [ ] Danger ends in relief
- [ ] Every sentence ≤ 4 seconds spoken
- [ ] CTA names who to send it to
- [ ] Truth contract passed (SKILL.md); sources in caption
