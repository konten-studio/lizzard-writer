# Twist Kadal

"Lizard brain" here is a shorthand, not a neuroscience claim: it stands for the fast, gut-level check people make before they think things through. That check asks two things:

1. **Danger mode:** "Is something hurting me without me knowing?"
2. **Happy mode:** "Is there an easy way to get what I want?"

Twist Kadal content opens in one of those two modes, then pays off with something **smaller and simpler than the audience expected**. The target reaction is:

> "Oh... hanya dengan begitu aja ternyata."

The surprise of "it's that simple" is the twist. It tends to raise watch time, saves and shares, and makes the next step (follow, join, click) feel easy.

## The formula

```
TRIGGER (danger / happy)  →  SMALL CAUSE or SMALL ACTION  →  "ternyata cuma gitu" PAYOFF  →  NEXT STEP
```

- **Danger twist:** a big fear, caused by something tiny and everyday.
  "5 kebiasaan kecil ini ternyata bisa bikin sendi lutut lebih cepat aus." Payoff: the habits are ordinary (duduk berjam-jam tanpa gerak, langsung olahraga berat tanpa pemanasan...).
- **Happy twist:** a big win, unlocked by something tiny and doable today.
  "Cukup gerakan 2 menit ini aja, lutut kerasa lebih enteng." Payoff: one simple move, shown on screen.

The contrast between **big stake** and **small lever** is the engine. Big stake + big lever = boring. Small stake + small lever = nobody cares.

Read [references/formula.md](references/formula.md) for the 8 twist patterns, the stake/lever grid, and scoring. Read [references/examples.md](references/examples.md) for worked examples across niches and unsafe-to-safe repairs.

## Truth contract (non-negotiable)

The twist only works long-term if the payoff is real. Lizard-brain content is easy to abuse, so:

1. **The payoff must be true and the body must deliver it.** Never tease a "simple trick" the content doesn't actually show.
2. **No invented fear.** Danger mode names a real, sourced risk. Don't inflate (`bisa` ≠ `pasti`, `memperburuk` ≠ `menyebabkan`).
3. **No miracle cures.** In health, money, and legal niches, the simple action is a *helpful step*, not a cure or guarantee. Say "membantu", "salah satu penyebab", "bisa memperburuk". Add "cek ke dokter/ahli" when stakes are real.
4. **Keep the source's claim ceiling.** Don't upgrade anecdote to science, one case to everyone, or possibility to certainty.
5. **Ad platforms:** avoid "kamu" + negative personal attribute/condition in paid ads ("Kamu kena asam urat?"). Use third person or general framing ("Banyak orang di atas 40...") for ad copy.

If the user asks for a claim that breaks this, refuse that one claim in a sentence and give the strongest true version.

## Workflow

1. **Lock the input.** Topic/product, audience (age, situation, what they fear, what they want), platform/format, language/register, CTA. Note the facts and evidence you actually have. Ask only if a missing fact would change everything.
2. **Map the stakes.** List 3-5 real **fears** (danger) and 3-5 real **desires** (happy) for this audience, in their own words.
3. **Find small levers.** For each stake, find small, concrete, everyday causes (danger) or actions (happy). Smaller and more familiar = stronger twist. Must be true.
4. **Generate.** Default: 10 ideas, mixed roughly 50/50 danger/happy, across at least 4 patterns from `formula.md`. Each idea = hook line + the "ternyata cuma gitu" payoff in one line + mode + pattern.
5. **Score and cut.** Score each on Stake, Smallness, Surprise, Proof, Path-to-CTA (see `formula.md`). Truth is a gate, not a score: fail = drop or repair.
6. **Script the top 3** (or what the user asked for) in the requested format. Default short-video shape:
   - 0-3s: hook (mode trigger, big stake)
   - 3-10s: open loop ("dan banyak orang nggak sadar...")
   - body: reveal the small levers one by one, show don't tell
   - payoff line: the "ternyata cuma gitu" moment, said plainly
   - CTA: one next step that matches the payoff (join, download, follow for part 2)
7. **Test plan.** Recommend publishing many variants, reading retention/saves/CTR, and putting ad budget only behind winners. Suggest which ideas to pair as A/B (same stake, danger vs happy).

## Default output

```
Audience + stakes (2-4 lines)

IDEAS
1. [DANGER · pattern] Hook — Payoff: "ternyata cuma ..."
2. [HAPPY · pattern] Hook — Payoff: "..."
...

TOP 3 (scored) → full scripts
TEST PLAN (3 lines)
```

Write in the audience's language. For Indonesian audiences, natural spoken Indonesian (not formal), and match the register (older audiences: polite and clear, "Bapak/Ibu" or neutral "kamu" as fits; younger: casual).
