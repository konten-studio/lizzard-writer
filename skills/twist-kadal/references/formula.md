# Twist Kadal formula

## Stake / lever grid

|                | Small lever (tiny, everyday, doable)     | Big lever (hard, expensive, long)  |
|----------------|------------------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| **Big stake**  | ✅ TWIST. "Ternyata cuma gitu."          | Normal advice. Feels like homework |
| **Small stake**| Trivia. Nobody cares                     | Skip                               |

Always aim for the top-left cell: a stake the audience feels in the gut, moved by a lever small enough to be surprising.

## The two modes

**Danger mode** (fear of loss): health, money, reputation, family, time, safety.
Lizard question: "Am I already in trouble without knowing?"
Twist: the cause is something you do every day.

**Happy mode** (desire for gain/relief): health, money, status, ease, love, time.
Lizard question: "Is there an easy way to get this?"
Twist: the fix is one small thing you can do today.

Pair them: the same stake makes a danger version and a happy version. Test both.

## 8 twist patterns

| # | Pattern | Mode | Shape |
|---|---------|------|-------|
| 1 | **Kebiasaan kecil** | Danger | "N kebiasaan kecil ini ternyata [big harm]" |
| 2 | **Musuh dalam selimut** | Danger | "[Thing everyone thinks is good/safe] ternyata bisa [harm] kalau [condition]" |
| 3 | **Salah kaprah** | Danger | "Selama ini kamu [common action] dengan cara yang salah" |
| 4 | **Tanda diam-diam** | Danger | "Kalau kamu sering [small symptom], itu bisa tanda [stake]" |
| 5 | **Cukup ini aja** | Happy | "Cukup [tiny action] aja, [big win]" |
| 6 | **Menit doang** | Happy | "[Big win] dalam [very short time] sehari" |
| 7 | **Tukar satu** | Happy | "Ganti [one thing] dengan [other thing], [win]" |
| 8 | **Yang dilakukan [group] diam-diam** | Happy | "Kebiasaan kecil [admired group] yang jarang dibahas" |

Patterns 2 and 4 carry the most risk of overclaiming. Use only with solid evidence.

## Scoring (1-5 each, after the truth gate)

- **Stake:** does the audience feel it in the gut within 3 seconds?
- **Smallness:** how surprisingly small/familiar is the lever? (5 = they did it this morning)
- **Surprise:** will they say "ternyata cuma gitu"? Would they have guessed it? (guessable = low)
- **Proof:** can the body show it (demo, before/after, source, visual)?
- **Path-to-CTA:** does the payoff lead naturally to the next step?

Keep ideas scoring 18+ of 25. Anything that fails the truth gate is out regardless of score.

## Checks before shipping

- Hook says the stake in the first 5-7 words.
- The payoff is stated plainly, not hidden behind "link in bio".
- The levers are concrete nouns/verbs, not "pola hidup sehat".
- Danger content ends with relief (what to do), never leaves the viewer only scared.
- CTA matches the twist: "mau tau gerakan lainnya? Join..." not a random hard sell.
- Ad copy: no "kamu" + personal condition; no before/after promises in regulated niches.

## Batch testing

1. Write 10-20 variants (mix modes and patterns).
2. Publish organically first; read 3s hold, average watch %, saves, shares, comments.
3. Keep the top 20%. Promote them as ads; make 3-5 new variants of each winner (same lever, new hook / format / face).
4. Drop losers fast. The winner tells you which stake the market actually feels.
